# Style directions

Each direction is a real design tradition with specific rules. Pick one and commit to it. Don't blend three. Adapt the palette to the business's own colors when they have them; the palettes below are starting points.

Every entry has:
- **Good for:** businesses it suits.
- **Type:** the lettering look, with Google Fonts for the layout route.
- **Palette:** a starting palette.
- **Layout:** how the layout is built.
- **Finish:** texture and finish.
- **Prompt fragment:** wording for image prompts.
- **Avoid:** how this direction goes wrong.

---

## 1. Swiss grid
- **Good for:** specialty coffee, modern bakeries, galleries, design shops, clinics, architects' events.
- **Type:** flush-left grotesque, big headline in bold, everything else in one small size. Layout fonts: Archivo + Archivo Narrow, or Instrument Sans.
- **Palette:** off-white #F2EFE8, black #111111, one accent such as signal red #E63312 or cobalt #1F3FBF.
- **Layout:** strict column grid; text hangs from a top line; one photo or shape cropped hard; lots of empty space.
- **Finish:** flat offset print on matte paper, no texture.
- **Prompt fragment:** "International Typographic Style poster, strict 6-column grid, flush-left bold grotesque headline, small text set in one size, one red accent, generous white space, flat offset print on matte off-white paper".
- **Avoid:** centering, more than one accent color, decorative shapes.

## 2. Risograph two-color
- **Good for:** coffee pop-ups, record stores, bookshops, zine fairs, natural wine bars, community events, kids' classes.
- **Type:** chunky grotesque or quirky display. Layout fonts: Bricolage Grotesque + Space Mono.
- **Palette:** two riso inks on white or cream paper: fluorescent pink #FF48B0 + blue #0078BF, or yellow #FFE800 + green #00A95C, or orange #FF6C2F + teal #00838A. Overprints make a third color.
- **Layout:** bold image in one ink, type in the other, with the overlap creating a third color.
- **Finish:** visible grain, slight misregistration, uneven ink coverage, halftone dots in photos.
- **Prompt fragment:** "two-color risograph print in fluorescent pink and blue on uncoated white paper, visible grain and halftone dots, slight misregistration between layers, overlapping inks make purple".
- **Avoid:** more than two inks, smooth digital gradients.

## 3. Wood-type broadside
- **Good for:** BBQ, farm stands, markets, barbershops, county-fair energy, hardware stores, oyster nights, beer halls.
- **Type:** stacked lines of condensed wood type in different sizes and styles, each line filling the full width. Layout fonts: Big Shoulders Display + Zilla Slab.
- **Palette:** cream stock #EFE6D2, black #1A1A1A, one red #B3261E.
- **Layout:** centered stack is right for this tradition; each line set as big as it fits, with thin rules between groups.
- **Finish:** letterpress with ink bite into the paper, slightly uneven inking, worn wood grain in the big letters.
- **Prompt fragment:** "19th-century style letterpress broadside, stacked lines of condensed wood type in mixed sizes each filling the width, black and red ink on cream paper, slight ink bite and worn wood grain texture".
- **Avoid:** Western or "saloon" clichés (wanted posters, rope, revolvers), unless it really is a Western bar.

## 4. Hand-painted sign
- **Good for:** delis, taquerias, pizza by the slice, bodegas, fish markets, auto shops, juice stands.
- **Type:** casual brush block letters plus one brush script word; letters outlined or shadowed in a second color, as sign painters do. Layout fonts: Ultra + Yellowtail + Work Sans.
- **Palette:** enamel colors on a flat ground: red #D7261E, yellow #F6C400, white, black; or green #1E7A46 on cream.
- **Layout:** big product name, price huge, a simply painted illustration of the item.
- **Finish:** brush strokes visible in the letters, slightly uneven baselines, painted on a flat board or window.
- **Prompt fragment:** "hand-painted sign by a traditional sign painter, brush-lettered block letters with a painted drop shade, enamel red and yellow on a flat white board, visible brush strokes, simple painted illustration of [item]".
- **Avoid:** perfect vector letters, a script used for everything.
- **Variant, rotulación mexicana:** bright flat background colors, outlined letters, a cheerful painted drawing of the product. Use it only for businesses connected to that tradition, and do it respectfully, not as a costume.

## 5. Bistro / brasserie card
- **Good for:** wine bars, bistros, bakeries with a French or old-world angle, dinner menus, prix fixe.
- **Type:** classic serif, small caps for section heads, italic for descriptions. Layout fonts: Libre Caslon Display + Libre Caslon Text, or EB Garamond.
- **Palette:** warm white #F7F3EA, near-black #222, one bistro red #A3212A or bottle green #2F4A3A.
- **Layout:** narrow centered column (centered is correct here), thin rules, generous margins, prices set small after each description.
- **Finish:** letterpress or simple print on heavy uncoated card.
- **Prompt fragment:** "classic French brasserie menu card, narrow centered column of serif type with small-caps section heads, thin hairline rules, one dark red ink on heavy warm-white card stock".
- **Avoid:** Eiffel towers, berets, "Ooh la la", curly scripts.

## 6. Kissaten / Showa café
- **Good for:** coffee shops, ramen or noodle shops, dessert cafés, sandwich shops, retro cafés.
- **Type:** rounded or slightly soft gothic letters, numbers prominent. Layout fonts: Zen Maru Gothic + M PLUS Rounded 1c. Use Latin text only unless the business supplies real Japanese text; never fake it.
- **Palette:** coffee brown #4A2C1D, cream #F3E6CC, tomato orange #E2552D, pale mint #A9D8C8.
- **Layout:** product photos on plain backgrounds in neat boxes, price in a circle or tag, a short tagline.
- **Finish:** slightly faded 1970s offset print, gentle yellowing.
- **Prompt fragment:** "1970s Japanese kissaten café menu, faded offset print, product photo of [item] on a plain pale background in a rounded box, rounded gothic numerals, brown cream and orange palette".
- **Avoid:** cherry blossoms, Mount Fuji, waves, or fake kanji as decoration.

## 7. Seventies supergraphic
- **Good for:** brunch spots, smoothie and juice bars, roller rinks, vintage shops, pottery studios, family restaurants.
- **Type:** soft, fat, slightly wonky serif or rounded bold display. Layout fonts: Fraunces (high SOFT and WONK settings) + Karla.
- **Palette:** burnt orange #D9622B, mustard #E0A526, brown #5A3A22, cream #F4E9D3, olive #7A7A3A.
- **Layout:** thick parallel stripes that curve around a corner, with a big rounded headline.
- **Finish:** slightly faded print, warm paper.
- **Prompt fragment:** "1970s supergraphic poster, thick parallel stripes in burnt orange, mustard and brown curving around the corner, big soft rounded serif headline, cream paper, faded print".
- **Avoid:** groovy clichés (peace signs, flower power) unless asked; rainbow stripes.

## 8. Bauhaus geometric
- **Good for:** music nights, gyms and climbing walls, architecture or design events, galleries, modern bars.
- **Type:** geometric sans, sometimes set at 90° or on a diagonal. Layout fonts: Archivo Black + Archivo.
- **Palette:** red #D52B1E, yellow #F2C200, blue #1D4E9E, black, off-white.
- **Layout:** big circles, bars and squares forming a composition; type locked to the shapes.
- **Finish:** flat screen print with slight paper texture.
- **Prompt fragment:** "Bauhaus-inspired poster, large flat circle, bar and square in red, yellow and blue on off-white paper, geometric sans type set vertically along the left edge, flat screen-print finish".
- **Avoid:** random scattered shapes, too many shapes.

## 9. Photocopy flyer
- **Good for:** punk or DIY shows, skate shops, tattoo studios, late-night food, bike shops, underground parties.
- **Type:** typewriter text, cut-out headline letters (sparingly), or one heavy condensed sans. Layout fonts: Anton + Courier Prime.
- **Palette:** black toner on colored paper: fluorescent yellow #F4F000, pink #FF8FB1, or plain white.
- **Layout:** high-contrast photo blown out by the copier, text tilted slightly, a strip of tape at a corner.
- **Finish:** photocopy noise, toner speckles, blown-out blacks.
- **Prompt fragment:** "black-and-white photocopied flyer on fluorescent yellow paper, high-contrast blown-out photo of [subject], heavy condensed headline, typewriter details, toner speckles and copier noise".
- **Avoid:** the "ransom note" look across the whole poster.

## 10. Flash food photography
- **Good for:** restaurants, bars, food trucks, and bakeries that want photo-led posts and posters.
- **Type:** minimal; one bold sans or serif line in a corner, or none.
- **Palette:** whatever the food and the table really are.
- **Layout:** overhead or 45° shot, one dish, a real table surface (formica, marble, steel counter, butcher paper, a tray), cropped tight.
- **Finish:** 35mm film or point-and-shoot direct flash, hard shadows, slight overexposure, film grain.
- **Prompt fragment:** "candid 35mm film photo with direct on-camera flash, [specific dish] on [specific surface], hard shadow behind the plate, a little mess on the table, cropped tight, film grain".
- **Avoid:** softbox studio lighting, shallow-depth-of-field bokeh, hands drizzling sauce in slow motion.

## 11. Engraved botanical plate
- **Good for:** florists, tea shops, apothecaries, gin and cocktail bars, herbalists, skincare, garden centers.
- **Type:** old-style serif with labels in small caps, like a natural-history book. Layout fonts: IM Fell English + IM Fell English SC.
- **Palette:** cream #EFE8D8, sepia black #2B2118, hand-tinted watercolor accents.
- **Layout:** one specimen drawn large, centered, with numbered labels and a caption.
- **Finish:** fine copperplate engraving lines, light hand-tinting, aged paper.
- **Prompt fragment:** "antique natural-history plate, fine copperplate engraving of [plant/ingredient] with numbered parts, light hand-tinted watercolor, small-caps serif caption, aged cream paper".
- **Avoid:** fake Latin or gibberish labels. Keep labels to the real text supplied.

## 12. Linocut
- **Good for:** bakeries, breweries, farms, cideries, bookshops, craft markets, seafood shacks.
- **Type:** chunky carved letters or a sturdy slab. Layout fonts: Alfa Slab One + Zilla Slab.
- **Palette:** one or two inks: black + red, dark blue on kraft, green on cream.
- **Layout:** bold carved illustration of their product or place, with type carved in a banner area or below.
- **Finish:** gouge marks, uneven ink, white specks where the ink missed.
- **Prompt fragment:** "hand-carved linocut print of [subject], bold gouge marks, black and red ink on cream paper, uneven inking with small white specks, carved block lettering".
- **Avoid:** too much fine detail, which linocut can't do.

## 13. Grocery circular
- **Good for:** sales, discounts, bodegas, butchers, markets, liquor stores, anything where price is the message. Use it on purpose and with wit.
- **Type:** ultra-heavy condensed sans, huge numerals. Layout fonts: Archivo Black + Archivo Narrow.
- **Palette:** red #E10600, yellow #FFD400, black, white.
- **Layout:** star-burst or tag shapes holding prices, products cut out on white, very loud and honest.
- **Finish:** cheap newsprint offset, slight misregistration.
- **Prompt fragment:** "supermarket weekly circular style, huge heavy condensed price numerals in red starbursts, cut-out product photo of [item] on white, red and yellow, newsprint texture".
- **Avoid:** being half-hearted. If you use it, go all the way.

## 14. Giant type
- **Good for:** boutiques, salons, galleries, launches, openings, single-message posters.
- **Type:** one word or number so big it crops off the edge; tiny details elsewhere. Layout fonts: Instrument Serif or Bricolage Grotesque + Inter.
- **Palette:** two colors with strong contrast, such as a deep color and paper.
- **Layout:** the word is the image; small info is tucked into a corner.
- **Finish:** flat, matte.
- **Prompt fragment:** "typographic poster, the word "[WORD]" set enormous in [type description], cropped off the left and bottom edges, small details in the top right corner, two colors, flat matte print".
- **Avoid:** adding an image as well. The type is the image.

## 15. Cut paper collage
- **Good for:** festivals, kids' events, community fundraisers, florists, summer menus.
- **Type:** simple sans, or letters cut from colored paper.
- **Palette:** 4–5 colored papers, saturated but matte.
- **Layout:** big simple shapes cut from paper (fruit, sun, a building), slight shadows where paper layers.
- **Finish:** visible paper fibers and cut edges.
- **Prompt fragment:** "cut paper collage, large simple shapes of [subject] cut from matte colored paper, visible paper fibers and slightly uneven scissor edges, soft shadows between layers".
- **Avoid:** busy scrapbook look, washi tape, doodles.

## Borrowed formats

Lay the poster out as another everyday object. This is one of the strongest ways to escape the generic look, because the format does the concept work. Pick one that connects to the business.

| Format | Works for |
|---|---|
| Receipt / till slip | Specials, price lists, café menus, "today's order" |
| Ticket stub / raffle ticket | Events, launches, gigs, tastings |
| Train or bus timetable | Opening hours, class schedules, happy hour times |
| Newspaper front page | Openings, big news, "we're moving", anniversaries |
| Recipe index card | Bakery specials, cooking classes, seasonal dishes |
| Nutrition facts label | Juice bars, gyms, "facts about us", ingredient lists |
| Seed packet | Florists, garden centers, spring menus, farm stands |
| Matchbook / bar napkin | Bars, cocktail menus, late-night spots |
| Boarding pass | Travel-themed menus, "new arrival", world-food nights |
| Laundromat / hardware price board | Barbers, repair shops, plain price lists |
| Library checkout card | Bookshops, cafés, study nights |
| Tide table / weather report | Seafood, surf shops, seasonal menus |
| Report card / certificate | Schools, classes, kids' events, "graduation" specials |
| Lost-and-found notice | Pet businesses, humorous promos |

Rules for borrowed formats: keep the format convincing (real-looking structure, real fields), put the business's actual info in the fields, and keep it readable. The joke shouldn't hide the date or the price.
